### Hi, I'm Adar 👋

Computer Science graduate (University of Warwick, 2026). I build AI systems for **Turkish language and Turkish organisations**, and I test where they break, what they cost and what I'd recommend instead.

My projects work like small consulting studies. I write a prediction before I measure anything. Then I compare the usual approach with the fix and report the numbers, including the ones that proved me wrong.

**Featured**

- **[Kuyruk](https://github.com/adarpalaoglu/kuyruk)**: a tested FastAPI job service for long Turkish AI text jobs, with four measured failure experiments. A heartbeat worker cut the texts lost in a crash from 20 of 40 to 0 across 30 runs. One blocking line turned a 7 ms health check into a 252 s one. The CI cache every guide recommends saved 0 s. Turkish also needed 13–24% more tokens than English.
- **[SafePlate](https://github.com/adarpalaoglu/SafePlate)**: my Warwick dissertation project. It finds food triggers in meal–symptom diaries and suggests safe substitutes, using Node2Vec embeddings trained on ~220K recipes.
